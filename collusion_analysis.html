Key Finding

Under the standard fingerprinting collusion model (1 victim + N-1 independent watermarks averaged), the extracted victim watermark correlation monotonically decreases with increasing N as the signal dilutes to ~1/N strength. The Hybrid framework consistently yields higher NC than the single-tile baseline across all colluder scales due to 64-tile spatial redundancy.
